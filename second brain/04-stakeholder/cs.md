# Customer Support (CS)

Front-line responders for user-reported issues. They are the best signal source for what real users are hitting.

## What they own

- Triage of user-reported bugs and confusion
- Categorization and frequency tracking of recurring issues
- Escalation path to engineering and PM
- First-line communication back to users

## How to engage

- Pull recurring categories before each PRD interview
- Loop in for any release-note review (they speak to users first)
- Share new feature briefs ahead of launch so they can prep canned responses
