# Customer Success Coach (CSC)

Owns post-sale relationship with existing customers. The clearest read on whether the product is delivering value and where churn risk lives.

## Contacts

| Name | Contact | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| _add lead_ | _phone / channel_ | Owns top-N account portfolio |

## What they own

- Post-onboarding usage health for paying customers
- Renewal risk signals
- Feature requests from existing customers (vs. prospects)
- Quarterly business reviews

## How to engage

- Pull churn-risk feature requests into the inbox before each planning cycle
- Loop in before any pricing, packaging, or migration change
- Share roadmap previews under NDA when applicable
